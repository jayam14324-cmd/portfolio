🎯 My Professional & Learning Journey

I’m Mohit Kumar, a B.Com graduate with a specialization in Accountancy and Business Analytics. I have been working with Jigar Transport Corporation since 2023, where I work with vehicle and garbage-vehicle data, prepare daily and monthly reports, salary sheets, and handle data entry and data cleaning. This experience has helped me understand how data is managed and used in real-world work.

I’m comfortable working with tools like Tally Prime, MS Excel, Power BI, R Programming, MS Office, Xero, and Zoho. I’m also interested in AI and new technologies and enjoy learning how technology can make everyday work easier and more efficient.

Currently, I’m preparing for competitive government exams, mainly focusing on Railway, SSC, and Banking exams. Along with my exam preparation, I want to keep improving my practical, technical, and professional skills.

I believe that consistent learning, practical experience and curiosity are the keys to building a successful career.
